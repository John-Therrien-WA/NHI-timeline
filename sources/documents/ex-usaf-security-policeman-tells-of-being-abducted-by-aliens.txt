Of those, one individual reports having seen two figures dressed in US military uniforms associating with Grays; another describes witnessing either humans or human-like entities in unidentifiable uniforms of some sort, also working with them.

While I understand that none of these reports from experiencers even remotely constitute scientific evidence, the occurrence of widespread and ongoing accounts of alien abduction is undeniable and, I have no doubt, this phenomenon will be accepted as “real” at some point. Of course, as an abductee myself, who has repeatedly experienced the physical reality of these amazing encounters, I am admittedly biased about the prospect of this realization materializing sooner or later, among our species as a whole.
