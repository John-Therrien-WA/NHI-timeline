          About this page

          What this is

          The stories told about non-human intelligence and disclosure on the podcast American Alchemy (host Jesse Michels, episodes), with the public documents they name. Each story is what someone said happened. The page records who said it, where (the episode and time code, or the document and page), and who else says the same. Stories, not facts: nothing here says a story is true.

          How to read it

          Each story is sized by how well it is attested, in three tiers:

            The score is counted from the tellings: how many independent tellers, of what kind, with what documents. It is never judged by taste. A score of 1.0 means as well attested as the typical story here.

            The institutional trust slider in the Legend sets how much weight an official record gets. Move it and the sizes change.

            Lines join stories about the same event, a variant of one, or one that corroborates or contradicts (red) another, or two that share a document. Pictures are of objects and places; a person's photograph appears only beside that person's own words.

            How to move

            Corridor: fly through time with W and S; click a story to open it. Globe: press M to switch; regions are patches, eras are shells above them. The Keys panel has the rest, and / searches for a story, teller or place.

            Corridor: drag to look around, two fingers up and down to move along time, or hold the arrows on the pad; tap a story to open it. Globe: switch at the top; regions are patches, eras are shells, drag to turn it and pinch to zoom. How to move has the rest, and the search box finds a story, teller or place.

            How to leave a note

            Open a story and write in Notes at the bottom of its panel: a source, a correction, another telling, or just what you think of it. For the page as a whole, use the box below. Notes are saved with the page, and a person reads them before anything changes.
