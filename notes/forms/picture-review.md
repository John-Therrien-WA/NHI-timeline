# Picture review
Nineteen pictures from the picture pilot that need your eye: stand-ins, thin fits and the fair-use images. Each shows the picture as the timeline shows it.
Keep leaves it on the page. Drop deletes the picture and its record. Replace keeps it until a better picture is found, and the few words under the choice say what to look for. While any fair-use picture stays, the page stays private.
